  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lizzulfiqar/llm-benchmark-tracker-20202026
  Downloads as a zip containing three CSV files:
    * frontier_models1.csv     -  80 rows x 32 cols - flagship commercial models,
                                  includes per-token input/output pricing
    * research_models1.csv     - 106 rows x 29 cols - academic/research models
    * community_finetunes1.csv - 100 rows x 24 cols - community fine-tunes

  released before GPT-3.5 Turbo (Nov 2022) predate public per-token API
  pricing; Arena Elo is absent for models predating the LMSYS Chatbot Arena
  launch (May 2023).
